Privacy

This page publishes host performance readings and aggregated history. Discord accounts, IDs, commands, IP addresses, tokens and logs are not part of it.

It uses no analytics, cookies, local storage or accounts. Cloudflare delivers the site and processes connection information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

Readings update about once a minute and history every five minutes. Old readings are marked as such. This is a home lab, not an uptime guarantee.
